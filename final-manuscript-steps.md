## Final Manuscript Steps

[Statcheck.io](https://Statcheck.io) — verify inferential statistics are internally consistent

[Reciteworks.com/check](https://reciteworks.com/check) — verify citations match references and vice versa

[OSF.io](https://OSF.io) — upload data, code, questionnaires, stimuli, etc. and create read-only link. Verify all materials are blinded for review.

[PsyArXiv](https://psyarxiv.com) — I usually wait until acceptance to post an identified pre-print, to maintain author blind, but you can post one anytime. First create it, and then generate a DOI, and then modify the document and re-upload so that it contains the DOI also. It will be indexed automatically by Google Scholar. If the paper is accepted at a journal, update the pre-print again to list the journal name and official, published DOI.

Make separate Word documents:

- Blinded manuscript with view-only link to OSF (for blind peer review; replace after acceptance with main OSF link)

- Supplement

- Title page with acknowledgments and CRediT taxonomy (e.g., pp. 11 of our [2021 replication paper](https://www.dropbox.com/s/qyusjlpls9c31ca/Brick%202021%20Self-interest%20Is%20overestimated-two%20successful%20pre-registered%20replications%20and%20extensions%20of%20Miller%20and%20Ratner%20%281998%29.pdf?dl=0)).

- Cover letter: very brief (e.g., 2-3 paragraphs): what we did, what the advance is). [2021 example](https://www.dropbox.com/s/tdofcp78xcei3kw/CoverLetter_Spillover.docx?dl=0).

The first author typically does the journal submission, but sometimes the senior or corresponding author will do the journal communication.
